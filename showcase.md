## 📌 Option 1: The High-Impact Storytelling Post (Recommended)

> **Best for:** Maximum organic reach, technical credibility, and engagement from developers and hiring managers.

```markdown
🚀 Excited to unveil NextHire — A Modern Full-Stack Job Discovery & Talent Acquisition Platform!

Most job portals today feel fragmented. Job seekers drop resumes into an opaque void with zero visibility, while recruiters drown in unstructured applications and messy spreadsheets.

I built NextHire to bridge that gap — creating a fast, transparent, and cohesive talent marketplace powered by modern web architecture.

Here is a look under the hood:

──────────────────────────────────────────────
💡 What is NextHire?
──────────────────────────────────────────────
NextHire is a modern, end-to-end recruitment platform designed with dedicated role-tailored workspaces for Job Seekers, Recruiters, and Platform Admins. It brings speed, clarity, and delight back to the hiring process.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
✨ 5 Core Features
──────────────────────────────────────────────
1️⃣ Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
Tailored workspaces for 3 distinct actors:
• Seekers: Search, save, apply, and track status.
• Recruiters: Register verified companies, publish openings, and manage applicant lifecycles.
• Admins: Moderate companies, audit job listings, and oversee platform health.

2️⃣ High-Performance Discovery & Smart Search:
Server-rendered job catalog with debounced keyword querying, category filtering, job-type toggles (Remote, Full-Time, Contract, etc.), and multi-metric sorting.

3️⃣ End-to-End ATS & Candidate Lifecycle:
Full hiring pipeline with real-time status transitions:
Applied ➔ Under Review ➔ Shortlisted ➔ Offered / Rejected. No lost candidates.

4️⃣ Real-Time Analytics & Dynamic Dashboards:
Interactive data visualizations powered by Recharts:
• Recruiters track candidate volume and post engagement.
• Seekers monitor application conversion funnels.
• Admins view 6-month growth curves and distribution metrics.

5️⃣ Enterprise-Ready Auth & Route Security:
Powered by Better-Auth with MongoDB adapter, JWT tokens, secure HTTP-only cookie caching, Google OAuth, and server-side route guards.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
🛠️ The Modern Tech Stack
──────────────────────────────────────────────
• Frontend & Core: Next.js 16 (App Router, Server Components & Server Actions)
• UI & Styling: React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, HeroUI, Framer Motion, Lucide Icons
• Backend & DB: Node.js, MongoDB (Native Driver v7) + Better-Auth Adapter
• Data Visualization: Recharts
• Notifications & Email: React Hot Toast & Resend API

──────────────────────────────────────────────
🔮 What’s Next? (The Future Plan)
──────────────────────────────────────────────
I am actively developing the Plan & Subscription Management Engine for both Seeker and Recruiter tiers:
• Seeker Plans (Free / Pro / Premium): Monthly application quotas, candidate spotlight boost, salary benchmark insights, and early job access.
• Recruiter Plans (Free / Growth / Enterprise): Active job posting limits, advanced candidate search, featured badges, and team collaboration.
• Stripe Billing & Webhook Integration: Seamless proration, self-serve billing portal, automated receipts, and usage-limit enforcement.

Building this challenged me to think deeply about server-side performance, session security, scalable database schemas, and intuitive UX design.
```
